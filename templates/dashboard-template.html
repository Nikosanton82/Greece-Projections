Official projections

Pseudo-out-of-sample evaluation, origins 2009-2024

Each model is re-estimated at every origin using data up to that year and forecasts 1 to 5 years ahead; errors are measured against the current vintage. RMSE is compared with a naive benchmark (last value for ratios and levels, 10-year mean for growth rates). The Diebold-Mariano p-value (HLN small-sample correction) tests equal squared-error loss against the naive forecast. Weights are inverse-MSE over horizons 1-3; where a structural block exists it holds a fixed 0.5 (it has no comparable OOS record) and the time-series models share the rest. Note that the evaluation window contains 2010-2015 and 2020, so absolute errors are large for every model.
